We'll keep adding 787 capacity in Charleston as our customers need it. The 787 remains the most efficient airplane in its class. The financials will follow our production performance.

It is so much more important that we do this right than fast. I'd much rather be a month late than a month early. We'll keep earning our customers' trust in every transaction.
- **Airbus:** Our core principles of safety, quality, integrity, compliance and security will remain at the heart of all we do. Today Airbus launches its next-generation single-aisle aircraft, for entry into service in the second half of the 2030s. We will invite CFM, Pratt & Whitney and Rolls-Royce to power it, so that our customers have a choice of engine and our ramp-up has secure supply. Secure titanium and forgings through the Aubert & Duval co-ownership and additive (DED) parts. Extend the critical raw-materials and rare-earths watchlist to NGSA. This covers our own ramp only and makes no claim on any rival's capacity [AX-0060, AX-0061, AX-0062, AX-0063].

The aim is supply certainty, not leverage [AX-0054, A-0243, A-0360].
- **Airbus:** Orderly A320neo family transition (Alt supply plan) — Keep the A320neo lines running longer alongside the NGSA ramp. The steps are a ceiling, never above today's rate, and NGSA has first claim on resources. The plan is re-checked at the 2035 gate and reverts to base on a supply crunch, an engine slip or a quality event. The cost is budgeted from NGSA entry into service.
- **Airbus:** Zero-for-zero trade advocacy — Work with the European and US industry associations, Boeing included, to keep civil aerospace on a tariff-free, zero-for-zero footing [AX-0088, AX-0046].
- **Airbus:** Skills for the Airbus of the 2030s — Run apprenticeship, graduate and university programmes in Europe to grow NGSA engineering and production talent from our own pipeline. This is recruitment for the Airbus of the 2030s and targets no rival [AX-0073, A-0330].
- **CFM/GE:** Offer a CFM engine for any new narrowbody either airframer launches, at minimum a LEAP derivative — H1: we never refuse an airframer and never lock one out [CX-0587, CX-0185]. This is a product offer only, with no price, terms or concession [CX-0184]. - **CFM/GE:** RISE stays funded as a technology programme with Safran: ground, durability and dust testing continue; no product launch and no entry-into-service date — H5: no cancel, and no programme dividend [CX-0174, CX-0398]. We fix the supply base we have before we load it with a new programme (lean before capital) [CX-0540, CX-0653].

It is sold, not given away, and it is not tied to any airframe campaign [PX-0088][PX-0100]. It has no effect on the game board. It keeps the fleet ahead of a new engine [PX-0080].
