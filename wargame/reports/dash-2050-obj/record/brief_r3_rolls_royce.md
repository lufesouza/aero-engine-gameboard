I think we're turning it. I don't think it's turned. It is so much more important that we do this right than fast, and we still have a lot of work to do.
- **Airbus:** Our core principles of safety, quality, integrity, compliance and security will remain at the heart of all we do. Our next-generation single-aisle aircraft is in its final phase of development and remains on track for entry into service in the second half of the 2030s, on the clock we set when we prepared it. We intend to offer a choice of engines. CFM, Pratt & Whitney and Rolls-Royce are all on the programme, and each engine will join the aircraft once its maturity and durability are proven: we will not compromise on certification. Until the new aircraft enters service, the A320neo and A350 families remain our priority, with a disciplined and controlled production ramp-up, delivery performance for our customers through the transition and continued work to secure our own supply chain, engines and critical materials first. We are funding the programme with financial discipline, within our financial flexibility and our existing shareholder return policy. We will continue to work with our industry partners for a tariff-free, zero-for-zero regime for aerospace trade. Preparing the Airbus of the 2030s means one well-prepared programme at a time, for a stronger and more sovereign European aerospace industry.

Let me tell you what we know and what we don't. Both new single-aisle aircraft have launched, and both customers chose to put CFM on them alongside others. We're honored by that. We also know we don't have a birthright on any of it. We keep a place on an airplane the same way we earned it: readiness, durability and support, one customer and one engine at a time. CFM is a 50-50 partnership with our partner, and we do this work together.

Our ducted engine is in its last year of development. It goes into service when testing says it's ready, and not a day before. Durability doesn't get traded for schedule. We won't give you dates or numbers our testing and our customers haven't earned. Commercial matters we work privately, with our customers and with our partner. You'll ask about competition.
