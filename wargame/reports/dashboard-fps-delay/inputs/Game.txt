CONTEXT
-------
This is a coupled non-cooperative game over the post-2037
narrowbody (NB) and widebody (WB) commercial-aircraft markets.
Two airframers (Airbus, Boeing) and three engine makers
(CFM/GE, Pratt & Whitney, Rolls-Royce) make simultaneous
strategy choices. Payoffs are EV-normalised yields (100% = no
change vs status quo). Equilibria are computed as Pure Nash
(no unilateral improvement) and Near-Nash (within an ε-band).
