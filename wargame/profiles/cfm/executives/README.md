# CFM International's leaders (GE side): executives and teams

**What this folder is.** CFM International is a 50/50 Joint Venture. GE holds its half through Engine Investments Holding Company [CX-0231], and Safran Aircraft Engines holds the other half [CX-0227]. CFM's own officers do not speak in any source [CX-0229] [CX-0230]. These files therefore profile the **GE executives who decide GE's half of CFM and speak for it** on GE's calls, and the teams they formed.

**For every seat, Safran holds the other half,** and Safran's leaders are not in the sources. Its CEO speaks once, on GE's stage in March 2022 [CX-0153]. A CFM programme, pricing or capacity decision needs the partner's agreement: narrowbody pricing "is a joint decision" [CX-0184]. Each team card in `teams.md` shows how that team handled the partner.

## Who uses these files, and how

**CFM/GE is not a player in the engine today.** Its engines are options the airframers choose (`python3 -m wargame.engine rules --scenario base --side control`):

| Engine | Segment | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `cfm_ducted` | narrowbody | The default for fps and NGSA |
| `cfm_open_fan` | narrowbody | RISE: +1.5pp margin, +1 year to EIS, 0.85 capture; no entry into service before 2045, so an airframe launched before 2037 waits for it |
| `ge_genx_next` | widebody | GE alone, not CFM; the default for the 787 Re-engine |

**The market cell** sets CFM/GE's reactions (bounded capture multipliers) from the public record. It does not read these profiles.

**The referee (game-orchestrator) uses them now** to judge whether a CFM/GE reaction is plausible:
1. Read `teams.md`: the baseline, the Safran gate and the default team's card. Use a historical team only if the game was set up with one.
2. Match the situation to the default card's thresholds. For example: in the game no open fan enters service before 2045, about ten years after GE's own window [CX-0608]; CFM/GE pressing an airframer to launch on it early and wait is implausible **(inference)** [CX-0205]; a 2023-24-style ramp promise should be haircut by a quarter to a half of its promised growth, with room for a 2024-type reversal when suppliers fail, while a low 2025-style guide may be raised (baseline rule 3; 2023 delivered +38% or +25% against +50% [CX-0590] [CX-0176], 2024 fell 10% against +20-25% [CX-0954], the 2025 guide was raised mid-year [CX-0654]); a CFM that refuses one airframer is implausible.
3. For detail, read the member's profile: § 2 (Quick card), § 3 (commitment track record) and § 5 (In the game, Part 1).

**A future `cfm` supplier player would use them** like the Boeing and Airbus strategists use theirs:
- each turn, run its team's ExCo script in `teams.md`;
- decide by the team's rule, with the Safran gate as a step;
- speak with the CEO's Voice lines.

Its levers are **proposed** and are not in the engine:
- launch RISE (`cfm_open_fan`) or an advanced ducted engine (`cfm_ducted`);
- a widebody offer (`ge_genx_next`, the 787 Re-engine default, or a GE9X derivative);
- standard or aggressive terms;
- a one-time LEAP durability upgrade;
- cancel.

Each profile's § 5 Part 2 gives that person's stance on each proposed lever.

**Plain-text role files.** `python3 wargame/profiles/build/make_role_txt.py` builds `roles/`:
- `cfm_ceo.txt`, `cfm_cfo.txt`, `cfm_coo_operations.txt`;
- `cfm_international.txt`;
- `cfm_leadership_teams.txt`.

The script reads this index, so keep its column names and the Teams year suffixes (one team per year). Re-run it after editing a profile.

## Evidence and checks

- **Evidence:** `evidence.jsonl`, 1,300 verified `CX-####` items:
  - the executives' own speaking turns on GE calls, December 2015 to November 2025;
  - 110 `cfm_jv` items on CFM International itself.

  There is no CFM company profile and no company evidence file. Each profile states the person's own doctrine directly.
- **Scope:** professional conduct only, drawn from what each person said and did as an executive. **(inference)** marks a step beyond the words.
- **Citation check** (0 missing required):

  ```
  cd <repo> && python3 wargame/profiles/build/cite_check.py <file> wargame/profiles/cfm/executives/evidence.jsonl
  ```

- **Citation audit:** `citation_audit.md` (2026-10-04) records two independent audits of these files, the fixes made and the claims relabelled as inference.
- **Isolation:** these files were built without reading Boeing's, Airbus's, Rolls-Royce's or Pratt & Whitney's profiles. Their views of CFM are not this company's evidence.

## Gaps

- **Safran's leaders.** No Safran executive speaks beyond one GE investor day [CX-0149] [CX-0152] [CX-0153]. Several things cannot be known:
  - Safran's pricing, capacity, workshare and RISE investment;
  - its view of GE;
  - any disagreement between the parents.

  Every Safran gate in these files is GE's account of the partner, and consent is assumed **(inference)**.
- **CFM's own officers.** Capital IQ names Gaël Méheust as CEO and President [CX-0229], and an Executive VP & GM of the CFM programme plus three other EVPs [CX-0230]. None speaks in any source, so none is profiled. Their parent of origin and remit are unknown.
- **No CFM financial model has been used yet.** The Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley CFM and Safran models are the inputs for a CFM company profile or a CFM supplier player. Until then:
  - no CFM-level P&L, capex or value per engine is calibrated;
  - every lifecycle number in these files is GE's half, in GE's words;
  - the ExCo thresholds are rules, not payoffs.
- **The default team's operating seat** rests on Capital IQ [CX-0232] and Culp's description [CX-0633]. Ali's own words end in March 2024 [CX-0013]. Who now works CFM pricing with the partner, as Stokes did [CX-0184], is not shown.
- **No engine launch.** No GE leader in these files has launched a new CFM product engine. RISE is still a technology programme [CX-0147], so launch behaviour is inferred everywhere.
- **Not addressed in the evidence:** an fps Joint Venture with Embraer, Delay Tactics and Poaching.
