The Board credits the CEO with the "seamless transition" of the CEO of Commercial Aircraft [AX-0093]; that quote refers to the role and names neither man.

- His prior career is not in the sources.

**What he inherited (end-2025):**
- **Rate commitment:** A320 family at "between 70 and 75 aircraft a month by the end of 2027, stabilising at rate 75". The Board Report attributes the slip to an engine supplier whose name is illegible [AX-0054].
- **Delivery record:** 793 deliveries in 2025, up from 766 in 2024 but 3% below the initial projection, after a 3 December guidance reset that the Board ties to supply-chain challenges [AX-0052, AX-0053, AX-0009].
- **Quality:** two technical issues at end-2025, with root-cause investigations under way [AX-0056], and A320 quality reports [AX-0057].
- **Capacity:** new A321 lines in China and the US, and the Spirit work packages for the A220 and A350 [AX-0060].
- **Programmes:** the A350F in its industrial phase [AX-0082], and NGSA technologies being matured, with no launch [AX-0020].
- **Market position:** about 60% of the single-aisle backlog [AX-0025].

**Evidence base.** 2 AX items [AX-0108, AX-0109], plus 2 that name him in the handover [AX-0100, A-0493] and 1 that refers only to the role [AX-0093]. **Confidence: Very low.** The profile is derived from his role.
