# Airbus key financials (for decisions)

**Sources**
- `evidence/fin_airbus.md`, extracted from the Airbus Board Report FY2025 (`airbus_fy2025`).
- The verified evidence items cited in the `Id` column.

**Conventions**
- Page numbers are OCR page indices, one higher than the printed page numbers.
- Amounts are in € million unless stated otherwise.
- "Computed" marks ratios derived by the extraction script, not stated in the source.
- No new numbers have been added. Only one year (FY2025 vs FY2024) is available, apart from the 2021-25 series in section 3.
