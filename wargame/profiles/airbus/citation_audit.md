## Hard rules relabelled as inference

- **Rule 2, "our own clock".** No item shows Airbus refusing to re-time a clean sheet around Boeing. The neo head start is context only.
- **Rule 3, no A350 Re-engine after a 787 Re-engine.** This rests on the game's Chicken structure. The historical analogue (the A330neo, a cheap re-engine against the newer 787) cuts the other way.
- **Rule 7, never cancel NGSA in reaction to Boeing.** There is no evidence either way. The A380 citation described a clean sheet, not a derivative.
