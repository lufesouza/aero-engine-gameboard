## 2. Quick card

**Objective function, ranked (his own list) [BX-0556].**
1. **"Fully restoring the health of our balance sheet"** [BX-0556], while "supporting our investment-grade rating" [B-2345].
2. **"The planned improvements of our recovery"**, made sustainable [BX-0556]. "Operational excellence will be the key to unlocking our cash flow potential" [BX-0555].
3. **"Keeping an eye on the future"** while staying focused on the short and medium term.

**How he differs from West and sharpens the doctrine.**
- **One conservative reset, not serial ones.**
  - "The reset allows us to operate to a higher confidence plan" [BX-0548].
  - He wants a baseline that lets Boeing "not only meet it but potentially beat it" [BX-0553].
  - This matches Ortberg's wish that the 777X not be "a continuous quarterly issue" [B-2342]. It is the opposite of West's early habit of reaffirming dated targets.
- **No inherited targets.** He said it was "a little early" to comment on a long-term framework, including the $10B FCF target, and gave no date [B-2337]. He deferred the 2026 numbers to the planning cycle [BX-0549].
- **More bullish tone, fewer numbers:** "things are trending favorably, and we're bullish on our outlook" [BX-0549].
- **Invests through the recovery.** Capex is "closer to $3 billion" for growth in St. Louis and Charleston [BX-0546].

**Decision rules.**
- **Rates step up only after stability,** "jointly agreed with the FAA" [BX-0554].
- **The 777X baseline carries buffer** "from a schedule and cost perspective" [BX-0553]. That he applies this to every baseline is **(inference)**.
- **The balance sheet comes first,** with $10B of revolvers kept undrawn [B-2345].
