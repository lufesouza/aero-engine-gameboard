# Stan Deal: executive profile (thin evidence)

## 1. Header

**Roles, as shown in the sources.**
- Executive Vice President, and President and CEO of Boeing Commercial Airplanes (BCA), in November 2022 [BX-0526].
- He replaced the previous BCA head in October 2019, in the middle of the MAX crisis [BX-1218].
- In early 2020 the CFO named "me, Dave, Stan" as the trio focused on the MAX and the balance sheet [BX-1628].
- The task framing dates his tenure to 2024. **His departure is not in the sources.**

**Era (company evidence, not his words).**
- **Inherited:** the MAX grounding, with the build-through-then-halt pattern [B-1642, B-1593].
- **On his watch:**
  - 787 rates cut and deliveries paused in 2021 [B-2123];
  - 777-9 production paused [B-2012];
  - Spirit fuselage conformance problems [B-2097];
  - Alaska 1282 in January 2024: the 737 frozen at 38 [B-2133], then capped by the regulator [B-2103, B-2236].

**Evidence base.** 20 items in his own words (BX-0526 to BX-0545) and 11 company items, all from **one event**: the 2 November 2022 investor day. That event was a prepared presentation with a short Q&A.

**Confidence: low.** It is a single snapshot of a BCA head 3 years into the job, in the post-COVID recovery. There is nothing on how he handled the grounding, the 2021 787 halt, the door plug, or how he worked with the CEO and CFO.

## 2. Quick card

**Objective function, ranked (November 2022).**
1. **Stability and quality:** "stability and quality will be the key metric we manage the commercial division" [BX-0539].
2. **Rates as fast as the supply chain allows:** "the supply chain really is our limiter" [BX-0533].
3. **Clear the parked inventory** "largely through 2024, a few that spill into '25" [BX-0534].
4. **Finish the derivatives:** "our development pipeline is full. It's absolutely full." [BX-0540]. That meant the MAX 7, MAX 10, 777-9, 777-8F and 777-8 [B-1964].
5. **Get back to double-digit margins and "well into 800 aircraft" around 2026** [BX-0530].

**How he sharpens company doctrine.** He anticipates [NOW]: stability before rate, and no new airplane before the current ones are certified [B-2276]. He differs in two ways:
- he named supply-chain capacity, not FAA oversight, as the binding constraint [BX-0533];
- he gave dated certification forecasts that later slipped [BX-0528].

**Decision rules.**
- **Rate breaks:** break only on consistent Renton output plus supplier stabilisation, with variability built into the low case [BX-0537].
- **Ramp lead times:** hire about 6 months ahead of a break; "post paper" at the new rate 1-2 months ahead [BX-0536].
- **Inventory:** more buffer stock as "an important insurance policy" [BX-0538].
- **Supplier insurance:** carry "excess capacity to bail out and rescue distressed suppliers". Cannibalise parts from inventory airplanes only "as a last resort" [BX-0535].
- **Rework schedules:** "bake a little extra time" into them; "cautious rate ramp-ups … out into that '25, '26" [BX-0541].
- **New airplanes:** none while the pipeline is full. Improve products already in service [BX-0540]. The 787 gets an enhanced gross weight version, not a new engine [B-1963].

**Risk appetite.**

| Area | Rating | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Technology | **Low** | Incremental improvement only [BX-0540, B-1963] |
| Schedule | **Cautious on rates, optimistic on certification** | Cautious ramps [BX-0541]; certification dates that slipped [BX-0528, BX-0529] |
| Balance sheet | Not in evidence | Footprint cuts [BX-0532] are the only capital act |
| Fixed price | Not in evidence | – |

**Tempo.** Slow and gated on his own system. He would not engage on the A350 [BX-0543].

**Capital allocation stance.**
- Used COVID for structural cuts: 787 final assembly consolidated in South Carolina; a BCA building "liquidated" [BX-0532].
- Fabrication slack and buffer stock held as insurance [BX-0535, BX-0538].

**Product stance.** Derivatives and in-service improvement [BX-0540]. The 777-8 passenger version waits until the end of the decade [B-1961]. There is no evidence on engines or Joint Ventures.

**How he reads Airbus.**
- "I'm not going to comment on A350. I'm more focused on my products." [BX-0543]
- "we'll get more than our fair share" of widebodies [B-1972].
- The 777-9 and -8 "really stand alone. There is no competition." [BX-0542]

**Voice.**

- "the supply chain really is our limiter" [BX-0533]

**Biases to display when the situation matches.**
