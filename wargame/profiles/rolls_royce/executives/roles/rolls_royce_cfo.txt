BY DIMENSION
------------

Priorities. On arrival she named four CFO priorities: integrated performance management, commercial
and cost optimisation, working capital and the capital frame. Growth investment was not among them
[RX-0229]. She ranks uses of cash as "safety, then ... the balance sheet ... Then ... shareholder
distributions" [RX-0242].

Decision style. She ties strategy to budgets and in-year intervention, and said this "was not done
well at Rolls-Royce" [RX-0226]. She plans for some initiatives to fail: "because some things always
don't work as you expect, the importance of resilience" [RX-0227].

Risk. A strong investment-grade rating equals 1-1.5x leverage, but she would "run with the net cash
position" 18 months into a transformation [RX-0245]. She measures resilience as cash breakeven
through a flying-hour fall twice 2019's [RX-0260].

Capital allocation (timeline).
- November 2023: capex plus R&D above D&A "across the period", against about £300m below in 2022
  [RX-0211].
- February 2025: "average above" D&A [R-0899].
- July 2025: investment up every year since 2022 [RX-0264]. More than £400m in H1 2025, including
  UltraFan [RX-0263].

Product and strategy. RR's flying-hour receipts arrive before shop-visit costs [RX-0230]. Repriced
contracts and time on wing raise cash per flying hour, "most of the cash benefit is still to come"
[RX-0257]. Renegotiated onerous contracts lift profit first and cash later [RX-0258]. The Trent 700
LTSA balance has not peaked after 30 years [RX-0259].

Operations. Her supply-chain response:
- 250 people embedded at suppliers and a Trent 1000 task force, which raised Trent 1000 output by
  about 20% in 2024 [RX-0254];
- a make-or-buy review and dual sourcing (turn labelled "Operator") [RX-0236];
- castings and forgings named as the bottleneck [RX-0255].

Her view of refurbishments shifted. In November 2023 she expected large-engine refurbishments to
keep growing to 700-750 despite time-on-wing work [RX-0228]. By February 2025 she expected shop
visits to peak in 2026 [RX-0253].

Rivals. She measures share, not rivals [RX-0262].

Communication.
- She commits to direction but not to straight lines: "most absolutely not back-end loaded"
  [RX-0216].
- She states a miss in one sentence [RX-0250] and pre-warns of weaker halves [RX-0265].
- She ties each result back to the Capital Markets Day [RX-0249].

Crisis response. Her only crisis was the supply chain. Charges were £410m in 2023 and £382m in 2024,
each shown next to offsetting contract renegotiation [RX-0235, RX-0255]. She presented the H1 2024
working-capital build as a choice, then pointed to inventory progress [RX-0240].

Team. She and the CEO previously cut $3bn of cost in a transformation together [RX-0232]. The CEO
defers acquisition screening to "the way Helen talked about" [RX-0106].

  make the grade" [RX-0188].

okay approximation" [RX-0170], noting that lower flying also pushes shop-visit costs out [R-0827].
