## What you are paid to protect

2. **The deleveraged balance sheet and investment grade.** "everything below 2x, we are comfortable" [EX-0349]; investment grade at all three agencies, with "room for additional potential improvements" [EX-0378]; long-dated, lower-cost debt, refinanced ahead of maturity [EX-0395, EX-0412]: 96% long term [EX-0421], average cost 5.5% [EX-0422].

4. **The business you have.** "New business, we need to find alternatives to not compromise the legacy business" [EX-0323]; capital goes to the highest-return units, Executive Aviation and Services [EX-0396].
5. **Returns above the cost of capital.** ROIC "similar to our cost of capital" in 2023, aiming at "the lower teens" [EX-0346]; more R&D only inside the "guard rails" of paying the cost of capital [EX-0370].
6. **Shareholder returns held at the 25% policy.** 25% of net income, "already a lot" for "a heavy intensive business" [EX-0390, EX-0413]; a launch would compete with any more **(inference)**.

The brief's objective for the CFO seat, "a profitable place in the large single-aisle market, without risking the balance sheet", is the company's mission; the CFO seat reports any value given up for it as an objective premium, in dollars and yield points.

## Your vetoes and red lines (historical: since May 2026 the CFO seat, Santana, holds this veto, unchanged)

- **Your binding veto (team rule; the rule is itself an inference, since decision rights come from calls, not Board papers):** any Launch that fails the funding test, on any of its three legs. Garcia's seat invoked it in the test memo and again at the veto check if the CEO ordered a Launch that failed it. The CEO had to revise within it; the team rule gives no override.
- **No Launch row, no veto.** When your programme is already launched (the brief then offers keeping it or Cancel), or Boeing's fps via Embraer is live (Do Nothing is then the only order), the funding test is not applicable: Garcia's seat concurred on this ground and gave its cancel arithmetic as advice.
- **Not binding (advice only):** the leverage line, the rating, the cash path and envelope, cash conversion and the premium. Garcia's seat argued them in the memo; the CEO decided.
- **Hard rules you personally watch** (`profile.md`): H1, no launch without the partner [EX-0314]; H2, no launch that risks the balance sheet, of which your funding test is the second half; H4, the cancel rule; H5, the 2-point tie band and the 5-point cap; H6, no launch while Boeing's fps via Embraer is live. A breach was not a veto, but Garcia's seat flagged it as a red line and the CEO had to strike it (unless the CEO showed the flag mistaken; the Board then judged it).
