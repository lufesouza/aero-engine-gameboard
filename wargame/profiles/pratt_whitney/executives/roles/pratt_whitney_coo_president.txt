About the operating seat
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

What the seat decides. The President of Pratt & Whitney runs the engine business inside UTC, later
RTX. The seat owns:
- durability fixes and engine standards;
- the supply chain and capacity;
- the MRO network;
- the split of scarce output between Airbus installs and the fleet;
- campaign pricing within RTX's rules.

Capital allocation, the dividend, buybacks, the balance sheet and guidance sit at RTX level with the
CEO and CFO (see mitchill.md).

  2017, March 2018, June 2019).
- Eddy: 21 own-words items plus 20 company-file items, from one event (19 June 2023).

$B, with gtf_upgrade in round 1 unless stated. They are snapshots, re-checked on a fresh run in the
2026-10-04 audit. They are not evidence and should be re-run each turn. A CFM/GE or RR engine counts
only if that maker launches it in the same round.

Quick card

   teens GTF aftermarket ROS by 2025 [PX-0117].

  For large commercial engines this is inference: he extends the GTF rather than replacing it
  [PX-0129].

  compensation terms stay private [PX-0113][PX-0112].
