# RTX Corporation Board of Directors: web sources (`pratt-whitney-board`)

Web research for the board profile of RTX Corporation, the parent of Pratt & Whitney. All searches were run on 2026-10-10 with the WebSearch tool; the container cannot fetch pages, so each web item records what the search summary stated (never a verbatim quote) and the result URL that supports it. A web item counts as corroborated only when a second, differently worded search returned the same fact (`corroborated_by` in `evidence.jsonl`).

The search budget for the session ran out after 24 completed searches (the 25th was refused), below the 25-45 aimed for. Nine of the 21 web items therefore have no second search and are used only as (inference).

## 4. What the searches did not find

- A published dollar threshold for board approval of capital spending or a new programme (PG-0049).
- The full 2025-2026 committee rosters and chairs beyond the Audit (Reynolds) and Governance and Public Policy (Work, then Oliver) chairs.
- Any rating-agency, proxy-adviser or press assessment of the RTX board's risk culture, or any board statement on a next-generation narrowbody engine.
- The 2026 capital-deployment outlook (buyback restart, leverage target): the search was refused when the budget ran out.
- Leanne G. Caret's background and joining date (not in any result).

## 5. Verification (2026-10-10)

- The verifier's own, differently worded re-searches of all 21 web items were refused: the session's web-search budget
  (200 searches per turn, shared by all agents) was already used up. No web item could be newly confirmed, corrected or
  dropped on a fresh search. The `corroborated_by` entries above are the drafter's second searches.
- Cross-checks against the repo's own sources instead:
  - PG-0041 (2026 slate of ten): the FY2024 10-K, signed 3 February 2025, lists twelve directors including Hayes and
    Winnefeld (new filing item PG-0062, `rtx_10k` p. 1107), so both left between February 2025 and April 2026. Hayes's
    exit matches PG-0043; Winnefeld's departure date is not in any source.
  - PG-0043 (Calio chair from 30 April 2025): the FY2024 10-K signature page (p. 1110) has Hayes as Executive Chairman on
    3 February 2025, and the RTX call transcripts label Calio "CEO & President" on 2025-02-19 and "Chairman & CEO" from
    2025-05-28.
  - PG-0044 (Reynolds Lead Director from December 2023, succeeding Paliwal): Paliwal is Lead Director in June 2023
    (PG-0001), on the FY2022 10-K director list and absent from the FY2023 (February 2024) and FY2024 lists.
  - PG-0053: the second search confirmed only the product-cybersecurity role of the Special Activities Committee (with the
    10-K's classified-cybersecurity sentence, PG-0033); the classified-programme-risk wording rests on one search.
  - PG-0045: the corroborated part is the lead-director designation rule; the no-fixed-policy and Governance-review
    wording rests on one search and is used only as inference.
- Not in any web item, found in the repo's Boeing transcripts: a Leanne G. Caret was named President and CEO of Boeing
  Defense, Space & Security in June 2016 (Boeing call of 2016-06-08, `transcripts` p. 1586). That she is the RTX director
  of the same name is inference and carries no claim beyond a background note.
